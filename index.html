    About this map

    The Pali Rebuild Map follows the ground-up reconstruction of Pacific Palisades after the January 7, 2025 fire. Each dot is a lot with a new-construction permit, colored by where it stands in the rebuild pipeline. Press play to watch the recovery unfold from the week of the fire to today.

    Methodology

    Only new-construction (Bldg-New) permits are counted — the ground-up rebuild of a home. Repair, ADU, pool, wall, and other permit types are excluded. The funnel is cumulative: a home counted as “under construction” was also submitted and issued.

    Data & disclaimer

    This is an open-source recreation inspired by the Palisadian-Post’s Pali Rebuild Map. Lot locations, addresses, and dates in this demo are synthesized to match the published rebuild totals and are not real permit records. See the README for sources and how to wire in live permit data.
